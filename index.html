Noether Symmetries of Lagrangians of Maximally Symmetric Spaces

Final year project, BS Mathematics, NUST

Noether symmetries of many different Lagrangians have been found, and the conserved quantities that come with them help us understand how a system behaves.

In this project, I found the Noether symmetries of Lagrangians on manifolds belonging to the class of spaces of constant curvature, also known as maximally symmetric spaces. The curvature can take three kinds of value:

Positive curvature
Locally spherical
Zero curvature
Flat, Euclidean
Negative curvature
Locally hyperbolic
